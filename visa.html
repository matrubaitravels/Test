Document Checklists

What documents do you need?

Click any country to see the checklist

🇦🇪 UAE / Dubai Tourist Visa

Passport (valid for 6+ months, min. 2 blank pages)
Passport size photo (white background, 35x45mm)
Confirmed return flight tickets
Hotel booking confirmation
Bank statement (last 3 months, min ₹50,000 balance)
Aadhaar card copy
Travel insurance (recommended)

🇸🇬 Singapore Tourist Visa

Passport (valid 6+ months, min. 2 blank pages)
Passport size photo (white background)
Confirmed return flight tickets
Hotel booking or letter of invitation
Bank statement (last 3 months, min ₹75,000)
Income tax return (last 1 year) or salary slips
Employment letter / business proof

🇹🇭 Thailand Tourist Visa

Passport (valid 6+ months, min. 2 blank pages)
Passport size photo (4x6cm, white background)
Confirmed return flight tickets
Hotel booking confirmation
Bank statement (last 6 months, min ₹50,000)
Travel itinerary

🇪🇺 Schengen Visa

Passport (valid 3+ months beyond travel, 2 blank pages)
Schengen visa application form (signed)
2 passport photos (35x45mm, white background)
Confirmed return flight tickets
Hotel bookings for entire stay
Travel insurance (min €30,000 cover) — MANDATORY
Bank statement (last 6 months, min ₹1,50,000)
ITR / salary slips (last 3 months)
Cover letter explaining trip purpose

🇬🇧 UK Standard Visitor Visa

Passport (valid for duration of stay)
Online UK visa application form (completed)
Biometric appointment at TLScontact Bangalore
Bank statement (last 6 months, min ₹2,00,000)
Flight & hotel bookings
Employment letter / business ownership proof
ITR last 2 years
Cover letter (purpose, itinerary, ties to India)

🇺🇸 USA B1/B2 Tourist Visa

Passport (valid 6+ months beyond travel)
DS-160 online application form (completed)
Visa interview appointment at US Embassy/Consulate
MRV fee payment receipt
Passport size photo (5x5cm, white background)
Bank statement (last 6 months)
ITR last 3 years
Employment / business proof
Ties to India (property, family, job) — very important

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

It depends on the country. UAE & Singapore take 3–7 days. Schengen & UK take 15–21 days. USA varies by interview availability. We always advise applying at least 4–6 weeks before travel.
Our service fee starts from ₹1,000 for Thailand and goes up to ₹3,500 for USA. This is separate from the embassy fee which is paid directly. We give you a full cost breakdown before you commit.
No agent can legally guarantee visa approval — it is at the embassy's sole discretion. What we guarantee is that your application is complete, accurate, and professionally presented to maximise your chances.
Embassy fees are non-refundable in case of rejection. Our service fee is also non-refundable as the work has been completed. We will advise you on reapplication options and what to strengthen.
For most visas, you can send documents by courier. For UK & USA, the applicant must appear in person at the visa centre / embassy for biometrics and interview. We guide you through every step.
